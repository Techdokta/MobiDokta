What you get on every file

  • Device identity verified — IMEI and serial read off the device and cross-checked, so you know the assessed device is the insured device.
  • GSMA blacklist & lock status — a device reported stolen is flagged before you pay a cent on it.
  • Full diagnostic — hardware, software and firmware, with a pass / fail / degraded test matrix your handler can read in ten seconds.
  • Dated photographic evidence — exterior, damage close-ups, IMEI plate, internal board condition, corrosion and impact witness marks.
  • Cause-consistency opinion — whether the physical evidence supports the reported cause of loss, with the reasoning shown, not just a verdict.
  • Prior repair & non-genuine parts — specifically documented, because that is what turns up later and reopens a settled claim.
  • Itemised estimate — parts and labour line by line, VAT separate, parts grade and lead time stated. Approvable as-is.
  • Economic-repair test — repair cost against researched pre-damage market value, with a repair / replace / write-off recommendation and the numbers behind it.

Honest disclosure — please factor this in before you appoint us

MobiDokta is an independent repair and assessment provider. We are not a manufacturer-authorised service provider, and our reports are issued and signed as an independent qualified technician's assessment. If any of your policy wordings require a manufacturer-authorised assessor, those files are outside what we can service and we will say so rather than take the instruction. We would rather lose a file than have a report of ours rejected on your desk. We also hold no financial interest in claim outcomes — our fee is identical whether a device is repaired, replaced or written off, and is never contingent on a claim being admitted.
